Securely manage your passwords, locally.

PyPass Manager is a modern, open-source vault that keeps your data encrypted on your own machine. No cloud, no subscription, no risk.

shield Privacy First

Most password managers store your data on their servers. PyPass is different. Every piece of data is encrypted using military-grade AES-256 encryption and stored only on your local disk. You own your data, and you own the keys.
